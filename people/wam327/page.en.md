## Lai’s Memories of the Forgotten Moon

Lai’s memories of Moon date back a long time. Lai had always wanted to write a page for her, but due to Lai’s own procrastination, it dragged on until now, in 2026.

In Lai’s impression, Moon was always suffering. During the more than half a year they knew each other well, she probably never had an easy time. Back then, she went to school far away from home, practically having to cross half of China, and her relationship with her family likely wasn’t very good either. Lai didn’t know what Moon’s daily life was like, but Lai felt it must have been very arduous. In her final days, she tried to leave several times, and in the end, just as Moon had anticipated, she succeeded.

Recalling that time now, it felt like Moon was trying so hard to find an anchor to support herself, but ultimately failed.

Lai writes these words down now to remember Moon.
