## Lait’s Memories of the Forgotten Moon

Lait’s memories of Lvnaria date back a long time. Lait had always wanted to write a page for her, but due to Lait’s own procrastination, it dragged on until now, in 2026.

In Lait’s impression, Lvnaria was always suffering. During the more than half a year they knew each other well, she probably never had an easy time. Back then, she went to school far away from home, practically having to cross half of China, and her relationship with her family likely wasn’t very good either. Lait didn’t know what Lvnaria’s daily life was like, but Lait felt it must have been very arduous. In her final days, she tried to leave several times, and in the end, just as Lvnaria had anticipated, she succeeded.

Recalling that time now, it felt like Lvnaria was trying so hard to find an anchor to support herself, but ultimately failed.

Lait writes these words down now to remember Lvnaria.
