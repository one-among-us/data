## Introduction

We have no way of knowing what connection Xa9 had to the trans community, but on GitHub Xa9 chose She/Her pronouns, so that’s what we’ll go with.

In her own words, she was a rhythm-game enthusiast who loved Phigros, Muse Dash, and A Dance of Fire and Ice. Anyone even slightly familiar with maimai can tell that her avatar comes from one of its characters, Salt[^1].

Salt, that is what “Shio” means, after all… And like salt, Xa9 was an indispensable seasoning in her friends’ everyday lives. According to them, she was a cheerful kid, easygoing and upright, and deeply loved by everyone around her.

She was also passionate about amateur radio, electronics engineering, embedded systems, computer science, and photography. She was a aviation enthusiast and a railfan too — truly “a complicated mix”[^2].

On NetEase Cloud Music, she drifted in a sea of EDM and ACG music, “a listener who doesn’t share much.” Once in a while, though, she would leave a little something there:

> I hope my music can bring everyone a tiny bit of happiness. Even just a tiny bit makes me super happy!

On June 28, 2025, she released her birthday song, *[Soda Pop Days](https://music.163.com/song?id=2720546025)*. It was her first attempt at AI-assisted composition, and she mixed it herself. The bubbles of soda intertwined with a summer afternoon dream, bringing a touch of coolness to the sweltering heat. Her listeners loved it.

And yet it was this very friend, a “complicated mix” who always struck just the right note of joy for others, who left the cool soda to us that hot summer and kept the unspoken pain for herself.

We have no way of knowing why she left. We only know that life has since lost a spoonful of seasoning salt.
