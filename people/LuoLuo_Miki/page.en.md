## Introduction

Luoluo was a gentle and kind person. She would feel sad because of others’ sorrow, and she would drive hundreds of kilometres overnight to comfort a friend in their greatest need. At an age when she should have been enjoying campus life, she was instead struggling to make a living.

She was slight in build, yet she carried heavy responsibilities on her shoulders. She once saved others, but could not save herself.

> “The happiest thing is lying in bed; the happiest moment is when the paycheque arrives.”

Carried along by work and life for so long… you can finally rest now. No more bearing it all.

## Part I: A Partner’s Memories — Dreemurr

I had not known Luoluo for long, but the impact she left behind is profound and hard to erase.

We met when a mutual friend survived a suicide attempt. At the time, she was muddled from an overdose and kept talking incoherently.

Luoluo was a lovely child, one who would grieve for others’ sorrow.

Her life was in fact always rather dull. She was just an ordinary delivery worker, perhaps fond of a bit of McDonald’s now and then, or playing Arena Breakout at an internet café in the evening?

As far as I know, Luoluo attempted suicide once a few years ago. Before that, her original host personality may have been very violent? But after the suicide attempt, the second main personality—the Luoluo we knew—became very soft and lovable. What happened in between, Luoluo never really told me.

Later, on July 6, we became partners. The way we fell in love was perhaps very conventional… most likely love grew over time. Perhaps at first we were both just looking for someone to keep each other company? But we truly fell in love with each other. And that, perhaps, was also the beginning of the tragedy.

Though those ordinary days were dull, I can think of one interesting little story:

Once, while Luoluo was sleeping in the morning, a new personality suddenly emerged—“Yiluo.” A completely male personality. That time it scared me badly. But after another sleep, Luoluo came back.

Perhaps this little story does not sound interesting to others, but this is the Luoluo I knew.

She was still the lovely Luoluo, and I was still myself, but our fates became intertwined. That period was the best time I have experienced to this day.

Yet good times are always brief. In mid-August, her psychological problems grew worse day by day. We had planned to meet during the National Day holiday, but because of her overdoses, she later mostly kept it from me.

On August 23, when I started school, she took her own life.

A lovely liar, a person worth my love.

I do not know much about the first half of her life, so I will not say more. She did not particularly want to leave anything behind in this world, but since she left me behind, I want others to remember her too.

You fool, you liar… wherever you are, I will always love you.

## Part II: A Friend’s Memories — Xiaomian

To the gentle you, Luoluo. You saved me, so I come to remember you and to mourn you.

Luoluo was one of my best friends. Like me, she was a trans woman.

At first, we met in a group chat. I do not know why I took an interest in her, but later we became friends. We had known each other only a few months, yet our friendship was like that of old friends.

What I remember most is that when I wanted to die by suicide, she drove hundreds of kilometres overnight to find me. At the time I felt an enormous shock; I could not believe she would really do such a thing. I was deeply moved, and her weight in my heart suddenly grew much heavier. But that day I still could not hold back my longing for the next life. After she arrived overnight, I stuffed my important belongings into her car, and that night I still chose to leave.

Perhaps because of her influence, I didn’t go as far, and so I survived until today.

She saved me, but unfortunately she could not save herself.

She was very thin, with hardly any fullness to her face. But it was just such a slight person who carried too heavy a responsibility on her shoulders—at an age when she should have been studying on campus, she was constantly busy working during her brief life, often exhausted, spending her free time asleep, yet still having to support a whole family. Carrying responsibilities that did not belong to her age, she was both strong and heartbreaking. For a time, we talked almost every day and stayed in contact constantly. Though I do not know why, I liked this person very much, and this feeling was hard to control.

When I learned that afternoon of the news of her death, I was stunned. Stunned that she had not told me, and stunned that our promise had not yet been fulfilled. Just as life was moving in a better direction, she left, and I could hardly believe it. She and Dreemurr were partners, and they were both friends close to me; they had agreed to leave together. In the end Dreemurr survived, while Luoluo left. Dreemurr is the last important person Luoluo left behind…

I will certainly cherish and care for this treasure she left behind.

Luoluo, you said you did not want to come back. Then, in that happy world, please watch over us. May you never have to return to this hellish world; may you reach a gentle place where you no longer need to bear such heavy burdens, and where you can rest safely.
