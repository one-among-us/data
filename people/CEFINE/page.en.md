## Profile

Every time I scrolled through Nimao's profile, the first thing I would definitely see was a notification that he had changed his avatar.

Judging from the very few bio statuses he left behind, he was probably a kid who really liked mecha girls and gun girls; just like the quote above, which was also a part of his bio.

No one could have imagined that someone so optimistic, who hung out in group chats every day and surfed the internet so intensely, bringing endless warmth to his friends, was enduring immense physical and psychological pressure behind the screen...

## Memories

### By: \_AloneStar\_

He and I knew each other from a long time ago. As of today, he has been on my friends list for four years, but I don't know him all that well. He seemed to never mention a word about his real life. However, I do recall him mentioning his illness once. 

In short, he said he would probably lose his ability to take care of himself soon. He was such a poor kid... Yet he still chatted in groups and played games every day as usual. He didn't seem anxious about it at all, and occasionally even shared some nice pictures in the group. You couldn't tell at all that he was a kid with a terminal illness.

### By: Arista

Nimao was a very interesting and fun person... I'm not good at describing my feelings, but in short, he was just very interesting. During the time we spent together, he brought me endless joy. “Very fun” is the highest praise I can give him.

### “See You Tomorrow” By: Heibuzai

It feels a bit late to write this now.

Nimao, I used to call you Maomao (Cat). After all, you used to go by Maomao, and I don't know when you started being called Nimao.

You were one of the earliest friends I made on QQ. I can't quite remember how long we've known each other, and it's no longer visible in the friend details—because there was a time I deleted a lot of friends out of spite. I felt like you guys didn't care about me, thinking maybe doing so would catch your attention. It was very childish. A long, long time ago, you were the one who added me and invited me to play together. I was very happy: I really liked you, and even now I remember all the times we spent playing Minecraft together. But because of this, I felt a sense of alienation. In this small world of QQ, you were always surrounded by people, and I was gradually pushed out of your sight. Or maybe it was just my own mind playing tricks on me, making me feel that you weren't as close to me as before...

I tried my best to fit into your world—asking about the games you played, making friends with your mutuals—even though I was a bit clumsy at it. But you left, on July 3rd.

Do you know, I will miss you a lot. I don't know what position I hold in your heart, or what colour it is, but Maomao, in my heart, you have never been just an ordinary internet friend. Ever since I started using QQ, this social media platform, you have been the most colourful and important close friend I've known. We might never know who you were behind the screen, but we will always know “Ni Maomao”.

I don't know what others think, but I will never forget you. You will become the unique “Maomao” in my memory. Let's see each other again tomorrow, even though it's no longer possible, my dearest friend.

## Departure

Nimao's departure was very unexpected and sudden... It was as if a drop of water had sunk into the sea, nowhere to be found, or like a candle that had burned down to its last wick. He completely disappeared from the internet.

At first, his friends just thought he wasn't feeling well and needed to rest for a while. But as time slowly passed, people seemed to realize there was a problem. It wasn't until his phone was taken over by his family that we learned his illness wasn't some ordinary sickness, but rather—[^1]

<div style="text-align: center; color: #d32f2f; font-size: 1.8em; font-weight: bold; margin: 30px 0; letter-spacing: 2px;">
Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis
</div>

It was only then that most of his friends learned that the optimistic Nimao was suffering from a terminal illness, yet he had still left his cheerfulness on the internet.

On July 3rd, Nimao sank to the bottom of the sea in his sleep. ALS made him unable to breathe on his own. He was tired and didn't want to endure the physical and psychological pain anymore, so he closed his eyes and left this world forever.

> “One day, we won't need to say goodbye; we'll only need to say goodnight.”
>
> Goodnight, Nimao.
