## Profile

Every time I scrolled through Nimao's profile, the first thing I would definitely see was a notification that he had changed his avatar.

Judging from the very few bio statuses he left behind, he was probably a kid who really liked mecha girls and gun girls, just like the quote above, which was also a part of his bio.

No one could have imagined that someone so optimistic, who hung out in group chats every day and surfed the internet so intensely, was enduring immense physical and psychological pressure behind the screen...

## Memories

He and I knew each other from a long time ago. As of today, he had been on my friends list for four years, but I didn't know him all that well. He seemed to never mention a word about his real life. However, I do recall him mentioning his illness once. In short, he said he would probably lose his ability to take care of himself soon. He was such a poor kid... Yet he still chatted in groups and played games every day as usual. He didn't seem anxious about it at all, and occasionally even shared some nice pictures in the group. You couldn't tell at all that he was a kid with a terminal illness. —— \_AloneStar\_

Nimao was a very interesting and fun person... I'm not good at describing my feelings, but in short, he was just very interesting. During the time we spent together, he brought me endless joy. “Very fun” is the highest praise I can give him. —— Arista

## Departure

Nimao's departure was very unexpected and sudden... It was as if a drop of water had sunk into the sea, nowhere to be found, or like a candle that had burned down to its last wick. He completely disappeared from the internet.

At first, his friends just thought he wasn't feeling well and needed to rest for a while. But as time slowly passed, people seemed to realize there was a problem. It wasn't until his phone was taken over by his family that we learned his illness wasn't some ordinary sickness, but rather—

<div style="text-align: center; color: #d32f2f; font-size: 1.8em; font-weight: bold; margin: 30px 0; letter-spacing: 2px;">
Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis
</div>

It was only then that most of his friends learned that the optimistic Nimao was suffering from a terminal illness, yet he had still left his cheerfulness on the internet.

On July 3rd, Nimao sank to the bottom of the sea in his sleep. ALS made him unable to breathe on his own. He was tired and didn't want to endure the physical and psychological pain anymore, so he closed his eyes and left this world forever.

**“One day, we won't need to say goodbye; we'll only need to say goodnight.”**

**Goodnight, Nimao.**
