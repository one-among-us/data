## Introduction

Juzhi was an incredibly sweet trans girl and an absolute angel. She was always taking small steps toward that beautiful dream, dressing up in feminine clothes and taking care of herself so she could always feel pretty. She used to share photos of herself in JK uniforms all the time—insisting that it was only because “she didn’t really have anything else to post”—and every single time, people couldn’t help but adore her.

She just wanted everyone to be happy. Big on using kaomoji, she was always blurting out quirky, silly little nonsense, teasing everyone lightheartedly, and bringing comfort and warmth to so many people she met. As for Eina… when Eina was on the brink of breaking down or ending it all time and time again, it was Juzhi who pulled Eina back with so much sincerity and warmth…

> If you have anything on your mind, you can always talk to Juzhi~
> 
> Even though Juzhi might not have best advice, Juzhi will listen to you really carefully (⑅•͈ᴗ•͈).:*♡

But behind those sweet posts was a great deal of pain, much of it caused by years of neglect and rejection from her family. Her parents actively tried to stop her transition, favoured her younger brother, and showed a complete disregard for her well-being. Piece by piece, it all took an unbearable toll on her mental health…

Eventually, Juzhi somehow got caught up in prescription drug abuse. For the longest time, the only place she could find any peace was inside those drug-induced hazes… but no medication could suppress her heavy depression. She’d be overcome with sadness for no clear reason, completely stripped of any drive to go on living. The way Juzhi put it, she felt shattered into a million pieces: like a book torn into countless little pieces, or a sheet of paper ripped into scraps. Grief, envy, anger, hurt… all sorts of emotions would wash over her out of nowhere, and vanish just as mysteriously.

> Feels like I only become normal after ODing…
> 
> It’s like a broken mirror. Drugs are the glue that pieces it together… once the drugs wear off, the mirror breaks all over again _(:3」∠)_

Back in March, after a heated conflict with her family that pushed her over the edge emotionally, her state—already fragile—collapsed completely. In deep despair, she brought her brief life to a quiet, sudden end，leaving her forever at the age of 17.

Looking back, there are still so many thoughts tangled up in my mind that I don’t really know how to put them into words. But I still catch myself missing Juzhi all the time. She was once Eina’s best sister…

> Even if your heart was frozen…
>
> We all still wanted to melt… yours.
