> Me? All I ever wanted in this life was to be “normal.”  
> A home without divorce, without domestic violence, without classmates who bully me, without a best friend who took her own life.
>
> The good news: I got to live the life you read about in novels. 
> The bad news: it’s a novel by Yu Hua.[^1]

Hime-chan’s story is a long one. A large part of why she left this world comes down to the one-sided domestic abuse from her parents.

She was terribly afraid of her father.

Because Twitter/X raked through her account after all this time and flagged old content as violations, her account was unjustly frozen — and even her closest friends find it hard to look back on that chapter.

Dear reader, if you still remember her, please leave a memory of her here.

[^1]: Yu Hua is one of China’s most celebrated contemporary novelists, best known for *To Live*. His works are famous for their unflinching portrayal of ordinary people enduring relentless suffering, loss, and tragedy.
