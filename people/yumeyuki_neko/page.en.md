## Introduction

Yumeyuki Neko was a trans girl from Guangdong. She was deft and clever, lively and cheerful, free and unconstrained, and deeply loved by everyone around her.

Yumeyuki’s first experience wearing girl’s clothing was in early 2022. In the [video](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1zZ4y1S7C3), she was filled with joy and unease as she carefully put on a JK uniform. The moment she finished dressing, she looked truly happy. From then on, she began the routine of livestreaming games in girl’s clothing every day at 3:30 in the afternoon, and gradually gathered more and more followers.

She also once shared her RLE journey. It took nearly three years, from losing weight, to putting together outfits, to getting hair extensions… Little by little, Yumeyuki felt her way forward, drawing closer to the version of herself she liked.

Yumeyuki loved life. She once went grocery shopping with her bestie Sakurayuki Little Fox, then bought her a skirt, and later secretly tried it on herself… Several of her videos capture the everyday life of the two of them. Those records of daily life were plain and ordinary, yet they are precisely the truest face of friendship.

She was also a multi-talented girl. As a part-time virtual streamer, her Live 2D model — from the original artwork, to the rigging, to the modelling — was completed entirely by her own hands. She also did cosplay; because she loved Venti, the bard from *Genshin Impact*, she cosplayed as Venti.

She even pulled for Venti’s banner while wearing that cosplay. But then…

> I cosplayed Venti just so I could pull Venti today!
>
> How did I get spooked! Tighnari[^1], explain yourself, Tighnari! Little fox, explain yourself right now — Little fox! What is going on!
>
> It’s all the fox’s fault! Because there’s a fox sitting right next to me! Waaah… hard pity…

Ahaha… never mind that. Just a little mishap…

About how she was addressed…? Yumeyuki was always well aware of the term “MtF,” but in most of her videos, she called herself a “femboy” or a “cute boy.” Perhaps it was to avoid throttling, so that more people could see her…

Sadly, in the winter of 2023, because her mother was estranged from her and threatened to cut off their mother-daughter relationship, she ultimately chose to leave on her own…

After Yumeyuki Neko passed away, many friends and followers who cared about her continued to leave messages in the comments, keeping her in their thoughts.

Her friend Huohuo started a video series, “Bringing Yumeyuki to See the World.”

> Yumeyuki has gone to the cat planet. We will become her eyes and see this world for her.
>
> We will definitely bring the cat to see this world properly. She will surely be able to see it.

After Yumeyuki left, what changes have taken place in the world? What new films have been released, what new games have come out…?

In this world, there is every day she did not have time to experience, every landscape she never set foot in, and every new character she never got to know… We wish so much that we could tell her all of it.

Yumeyuki Neko, we will always be waiting for you to come back.

[^1]: Tighnari: A character in *Genshin Impact*, based on the fennec fox.
