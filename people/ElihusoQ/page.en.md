<h3 align = "center">I: We were always so good at meeting, and never thought about parting.</h3>

<p style="text-align: center;">Beiyan Maple</p>

We were always so good at meeting, and never thought about parting. When we happen to meet, and when we can write a story for it and leave behind a memory, we think there will always be time later. We never think that this might be the last time we ever see each other.

The first and last time I met Elihuso was at a Saizeriya in Nanjing. I had come to Nanjing that day to meet a few trans friends, and by chance learned that one of them knew Elihuso. So I asked her whether she could put me in touch with Elihuso — I wanted to meet her, really just to fulfil a dream of meeting an idol. After getting Elihuso’s consent, I arranged dinner with her that afternoon. We planned to meet at the Saizeriya on Xinghuo Road, but my family had bought me an 8 p.m. ticket and insisted I go back. In the end we could only meet for a moment at the subway entrance. We hugged, made a promise to meet again in October, and then hurriedly parted…

Elihuso was a very gentle, very good big sister. She had a kind of enthusiasm that can be a little excessive, but never made you feel offended…

There is actually a lot more I want to say… but I don’t know what to keep saying… I hope she can have a good sleep…

R. I. P.

May we meet again in the next life by the subway entrance on Xinghuo Road… and listen together to that one part of *These Days of East Beijing Road*.

<h3 align = "center">Archive: A Letter to Everyone</h3>

<p style="text-align: center;">A Rabbit and a Kitten</p>

Since the beginning of 2025, Elihuso had been tormented by stubborn insomnia. She had tried many medications, yet it was hard to obtain true peace.

Later, she tried medications that seemed to her more effective, and also more dangerous. For a time, this rekindled her hope, but the accompanying problem of drug dependence also deepened step by step.

In the long struggle with her physical problems, her friends never left her. They did their best to help her control her drug use, to ease her sleep problems, and they even took her to see doctors in search of safer treatment. Yet her own resolve remained as it was. As she once told us, what she was doing was not simply out of a wish to “control” her condition, but was “heading all the way towards the abyss.”

Finally, on July 15 of this year, an accident occurred during her use of medication, and her fragile body could no longer bear it. During the subsequent resuscitation, she experienced respiratory depression and cardiopulmonary arrest. Although she recovered somewhat in later treatment, the damage from severe cerebral hypoxia eventually became irreversible. Her parents, upon hearing the news, came to Nanjing and took part in caring for her, and in early August they brought her back to Hebei for treatment. The effects of complications began to worsen at the end of August.

During this time of torment, we thought about the things from when she was still by our side:

Since the end of 2022, Elihuso had endured a great deal. She lost someone she loved, and she lost hope in many parts of the world.
Although people tried to stay with her, wanting to lead her towards the light, she advised us that “there is really no need to put your energy into her.”
And yet she truly gave us countless warmth: she often thought of others, brought hope to others, and even on her darkest days kept the kindness that belonged to her.
At the same time, we know well that she also had a sharp and true side, one that would even hurt others for the sake of what she held to in her heart.
We still cannot tell right from wrong in all of it. It is simply that these two sides of her character were always one.

Even though we always awaited the arrival of a miracle, the absence of miracles is itself one of the reasons we call them miracles.
In late September, after a long and stubborn struggle of her faint consciousness, she finally died of complications, and in that abyss she had once overlooked, she was reunited with Ani and other companions whom she had once deeply loved and lost.
But compared with her departure itself, we deeply believe what she once said: that she hoped we would live well on her behalf.
Though she is gone, in this world she has already left, her truth and her goodness remain deeply engraved in our hearts, becoming strength for us as we continue to face this complicated world.

May no one be troubled by sleep problems any more.  
May everyone walk side by side with those they cherish.   
May everyone still feel hope in this complicated world.
