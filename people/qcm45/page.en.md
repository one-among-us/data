> Here are some memories recorded, yet there is also a story that may be missing and can never be completed again.
>
> All of us (by which I mean all of us, both normal and not-so-normal) are imperfect human beings living in an imperfect world. We don’t live with the mechanical precision of a bank account or by measuring all our lines and angles with rulers and protractors. Am I right?[^1]

## A Memory

Although I never actually met Mengmeng in person, in my impression… she was a kind and lovely child who always cared about others.

Mengmeng loved *BanG Dream*, and it was within this community that I got to know her, not too early and not too late. At that time, [Darker](../profile/execute_darker) had not yet been sent to a conversion school, and the three of them were living fairly happily.

Mengmeng told me that their relationship was stable precisely because there were **three of them**.

In their daily lives, there were cat cafes friendly to trans people, calm and warm cappuccinos, hot chicken leg rice, and occasionally times taking photos together. But Mengmeng was somewhat introverted and didn’t often take the initiative to share their daily life, always shyly reposting those photos full of happiness.

Not long after, Mengmeng suddenly told me that Darker had been sent to a conversion school by her family.

I was astonished, but then calmed down, because I myself was a victim of conversion schools. So while Mengmeng and Shiroshita[^2] went to Guangxi and tried various means to get her out, I was also trying to contact Darker’s parents to communicate, and thus managed to add Darker’s mother on WeChat. The chatting was more or less okay, but there never seemed to be any actual progress.

Darker suddenly disappeared from among the three, and Mengmeng and Shiroshita were constantly arguing. In the end, she walked alone into the deep waters of the river at the end of August…

That night, she didn’t say goodnight to me, just told me she couldn’t take it anymore.

## Another Memory

When you get down to it, Mengmeng was actually my fan from the very beginning, you know.

She once had a boyfriend, though it was actually one-sided love—“I treat you as a very good friend, but you’re in love with me.” She loved him too much, but he couldn’t accept her as a boy. So that relationship became the turning point for her to make up her mind to become a girl. But the good times didn’t last long, and they later separated. Mengmeng then felt that life had lost its meaning.

It was also at that time that I happened to be writing practical tutorials about tinkering with devices on the Coolapk forum. Because one of my articles gained a lot of traffic and received an official push, I took the opportunity to establish my own fan group. Mengmeng, well, happened to also like researching kernels and developing Magisk modules, so she came into my group chat and chatted with me very well. So she gave her heart to me and fell in love with me, and afterward we naturally became a couple.

Until July 2023, when I couldn’t stand my family’s control over me anymore, I decided to go south to Suzhou to find her…

## Epilogue

“Those two… are children who will never grow up. And now they never will.”

“Will you miss them?”
