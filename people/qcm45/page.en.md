> Here are some memories recorded, yet there is also a story that may be missing and can never be completed again.
>
> All of us (by which I mean all of us, both normal and not-so-normal) are imperfect human beings living in an imperfect world. We don’t live with the mechanical precision of a bank account or by measuring all our lines and angles with rulers and protractors. Am I right?[^1]

## A Memory

Although I never actually met Mengmeng in person, in my impression… she was a kind and lovely child who always cared about others.

Mengmeng loved *BanG Dream!*, and it was within this community that I got to know her—not too early, and not too late. At that time, [Darker](../profile/execute_darker) had not yet been sent to a conversion school, and the three of them were living fairly happily.

Mengmeng told me that their relationship was stable precisely because there were **three of them**.

In their daily lives, there were cat cafes friendly to trans people, calm and warm cappuccinos, hot chicken leg rice, and occasionally times taking photos together. But Mengmeng was somewhat introverted and didn’t often take the initiative to share their daily life, always shyly reposting those photos full of happiness.

Not long after, Mengmeng suddenly told me that Darker had been sent to a conversion school by her family.

I was astonished, but then calmed down, because I myself was a victim of conversion schools. So while Mengmeng and Shiroshita[^2] went to Guangxi and tried various means to get her out, I was also trying to contact Darker’s parents to communicate, and managed to add Darker’s mother on WeChat. Our conversations were generally okay, but there never seemed to be any actual progress.

Darker’s sudden disappearance left a void among the three of them, and Mengmeng and Shiroshita argued constantly. In the end, she walked alone into the deep waters of the river at the end of August…

That night, she didn’t say goodnight to me; she just told me she couldn’t take it anymore.

## Another Memory

When you get down to it, Mengmeng was actually my fan from the very beginning, you know.

She once had a boyfriend, though it was actually a one-sided love—“I treat you as a very good friend, but you’re in love with me.” She loved him so much, but he couldn’t return her feelings while she was still a boy. That unrequited love became the catalyst that made her resolve to become a girl. But the good times didn’t last, and they later parted ways. Mengmeng then felt that her life had lost all meaning.

It was around that time that I happened to be writing practical device-modding tutorials on the Coolapk forum. Because one of my articles garnered a lot of traffic and was officially featured, I took the opportunity to start my own fan group chat. Mengmeng, who happened to also love tinkering with kernels and developing Magisk modules, joined my group. We clicked instantly. She gave her heart to me and fell in love, and afterward, we naturally became a couple.

That was until July 2023, when, unable to endure my family’s strict control any longer, I decided to head south to Suzhou to find her…

## Epilogue

“Will you miss them?”
