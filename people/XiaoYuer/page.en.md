Xiaoyu’er was a bus enthusiast and train enthusiast from Nanjing, and she also wanted, like other transgender people, to make herself look good.

> Xiaoyu’er—it seems quite a few people use this screen name too.

## The Sudden Bad News

> A message, a phone call, a person’s silent silence. Only the songs in the earphones kept playing on and on.

On October 11, 2026, LinWaterMoon woke up as usual—a little early that day, awake by 7:30. He picked up his phone to check messages as usual, and saw the message Xiaoyu’er had sent at 2:30 in the morning: “Goodnight. Only you won’t save me.” Along with several photos of chemical reagent bottles.

At noon that day, LinWaterMoon also received a call from a police officer in Nanjing… From that moment on, the music in his earphones did not stop all day…

In Xiaoyu’er’s suicide note, she wrote that for about four years she had longed to have a good-looking face, longed to become a cute, liked girl, and even wanted to become a femboy or to live as a trans girl. But in reality, she felt extreme disgust and inferiority about her facial features, voice, body, and body hair, and even saw herself as a “water-colour-scheme femboy exam-grub,”[^1] utterly unworthy of being a trans girl. She thought she was very ugly, and every time she saw a good-looking femboy online, or a good-looking girl, she would feel intense longing. She hoped to change her situation by wearing nice clothes and cosplaying anime characters, but because she was not good at expressing herself and did not dare to, and because she was even more afraid of others noticing the change and of her family’s obstruction and opposition, she eventually gave up trying. She once dreamed that her HRT was discovered, and dreamed that her mother said to her: “If you keep going, I’ll kill you!” But she insisted on continuing, and so in the dream she was killed. It may sound somewhat ridiculous—but she really did have such a dream.

Her suicide was not for the sake of a better reincarnation in the next life either; she simply wanted to end it all.

> If the process cannot be chosen, and it is not a process you like, then it is better to end everything early.
>
> Written like a pile of shit—I know I write badly, but this is all I can manage.
>
> Goodnight, world.

In the early hours of October 11, 2026, she finally decided to leave this world and end it all.

## The Parents Came Looking

Not many days later, LinWaterMoon met Xiaoyu’er’s parents—her mum and dad came together. “I’d better step out for a bit,” her mother choked out as soon as she sat down. “It’s still a bit hard for me to hear…”

“He (sic.) must have been quite brave—quite resolute. He probably had no more attachment to this world… In recent years he was on and off away from school, then back again; he had few friends—maybe because he was rather self-conscious by nature. He didn’t really want to depend on the family, and he didn’t want the family’s money; he wanted to earn money by his own ability… He cried and told me some of what was in his heart. Before, he was afraid his family would object, so he didn’t dare say it. He said he wanted to keep a cat, and he also wanted to make himself pretty…” Xiaoyu’er’s father said calmly.

Xiaoyu’er once said that she and LinWaterMoon were very much alike—very much alike in personality, yet also not quite alike.

> “I like LinWaterMoon the most,” Xiaoyu’er once said.

A few days before Xiaoyu’er left, she said she wanted to keep a cat. Her family agreed—they would buy her a cat to keep. They had planned to go on the 11th to buy the cat she had chosen and bring it home—but that cat would never again wait for the owner who belonged to it.

## A Hastily Written Ending

That day, the cat was still curled up in the pet shop, while Xiaoyu’er was forever stopped in that freezing early morning. She took with her all the struggles and self-loathing she could never speak aloud, and left this world with nothing but that softly spoken “Goodnight,” “Only you won’t save me.” The sun rose as usual, but it could no longer reach the gloom in Xiaoyu’er’s heart. And for those who cared about her, this calm farewell is a wound that will never heal.

[^1]: Water-Colour-Scheme Femboy Exam-Grub: This is Xiaoyu’er’s self-mocking compound term. “Water-Colour-Scheme” refers to a pale-blue/water-blue colour scheme in clothing; “femboy” is an internet/ACG term for a boy who presents as a cute girl, “exam grub” s a derogatory China mainland term for someone who does nothing but grind exam questions. Combined, it evokes a stereotype within that circle: an exam-grinding femboy who, even dressed in a water-blue outfit, is mocked as having poor “beauty sense” and as unable to shed the exam-grinder’s aura. Xiaoyu’er used it to put herself down—feeling that however much she wanted to be a pretty trans girl, she was essentially just an “exam grub,” beyond saving even by nice clothes.
