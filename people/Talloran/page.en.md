## Entering the Community

In the summer of 2025, Talloran connected with peers in the transgender community at an in-person gathering. During the initial stage of her involvement, she showed a strong desire to explore her transgender identity. With the support of peers, she obtained a diagnosis related to gender dysphoria and gradually began learning about fashion and styling within the community.

As a long-time fan of ACG (anime, comics, and games) culture, Talloran invested a great deal of time and money in this hobby, viewing it as an important way to integrate into the community. For example, she once spent an entire month’s salary on merchandise such as “goods.” This was also seen as an important outlet for relieving the pressures of everyday life. According to accounts, Talloran worked in a highly conservative environment where she was repeatedly required to present herself in a masculine way. This caused her considerable distress. On one occasion, with a friend’s help, she bought a men’s wig to wear over her long hair during work hours, so as to conceal her appearance and avoid pressure from her surroundings.

At the same time, Talloran showed genuine concern for the difficulties of her peers. For instance, upon learning that some peers were under financial pressure regarding gender-affirming surgery, she verbally expressed a willingness to provide substantial financial assistance.

However, as her interactions with community peers deepened, her long-standing psychological repression, confusion regarding her self-identity, and lack of awareness of healthy boundaries in intimate relationships led her into interpersonal difficulties after she entered the community.

## Harm and Being Harmed; Suicide and Rescue

The deterioration of Talloran’s relationships with those around her began with an incident involving the violation of physical boundaries.

During an in-person gathering in the winter of 2025, Talloran misjudged the nature and degree of intimacy in her relationship with another peer. Without obtaining consent, she initiated physical contact and attempted to force a kiss. This was met with rejection and a defensive response.

The following day, Talloran apologized to the person involved via social media and then attempted suicide by ingesting poison. Upon learning of this, several peers mobilized urgently. They located her residence through various channels, contacted local police and emergency services, and coordinated a cross-city rescue. In the end, they accompanied her in an ambulance to a higher-level medical facility for emergency treatment, saving her life.

During her hospitalization and recovery, more than a dozen peers took turns caring for her. However, this collective attention further deepened Talloran’s psychological dependence and emotional projection, placing those involved in her care under intense moral pressure and eventually leaving them mentally exhausted.

## Freedom and Existence

In the months that followed, Talloran did not receive timely and effective medical intervention. Instead, she fell into a vicious cycle of “pursuing the freedom of self-destruction.”

In the spring of 2026, Talloran repeatedly tried to assert her personal worth through actions such as making large public financial transfers and conspicuously giving away expensive gifts. When these gestures were rejected, her emotional state became increasingly extreme. She began frequently appearing at in-person gatherings while carrying dangerous items. Before her birthday or other significant dates, she also repeatedly announced publicly that she would go to public places to demand an explanation, causing widespread psychological panic.

During one in-person confrontation, Talloran displayed extreme behavior for the first time: she attempted to draw a weapon she was carrying in order to commit violence. Peers at the scene immediately intervened, disarmed her, and prevented her from harming herself or others.

In a later incident, Talloran suddenly rushed into the back-of-house area of a public establishment, seized a kitchen knife, and held it against her own throat. Peers at the scene decisively used physical force to subdue her, and with the assistance of others, they successfully neutralized the immediate threat to personal safety and prevented any casualties.

After repeated armed confrontations failed, Talloran did not stop her dangerous behavior. Instead, she purchased several more weapons of the same type—claiming a total of eight purchases—and publicly displayed screenshots of the transactions. This escalating emotional outpouring disrupted the daily operations of public spaces and caused severe stress reactions and PTSD among more than a dozen peers.

## Ending

Faced with Talloran’s escalating threats of violence and suicide, her peers attempted various courses of action.

On the one hand, they repeatedly planned or tried to contact Talloran’s parents and other relatives in her hometown, hoping that family intervention would encourage her to seek formal psychological and psychiatric treatment. On the other hand, some peers attempted to introduce professional mental-health intervention by contacting authorities in her hometown while avoiding contact with her parents.

However, they had long relied too heavily on non-professional peer support to handle a severe mental health crisis. When Talloran repeatedly showed clear signs of suicidal intent and violent tendencies, they worried that contacting the police or her family might lead to forced “coming out” or cause further familial harm. At critical moments—such as whether to call the police or seek hospitalization—they struggled to make a decision, repeatedly weighed the options, and disagreed seriously among themselves. In the end, they failed to reach a consensus or take effective action. This excessive concern over the risks of “coming out” also caused Talloran to miss the final opportunity for intervention by professional psychiatric services.

In early September 2026, Talloran publicly posted comments on social media concerning the risk of falling from a height. After that, all of her social media and gaming accounts ceased activity.

## Afterword

Talloran’s passing is a heartbreaking tragedy. As a transgender person living in a marginalized environment, she endured inner pain, identity anxiety, and psychological suffering during her lifetime, while also facing enormous pressure from family oppression and social discrimination.

While her death is deeply mourned, remembering the deceased does not require us to conceal the truth behind the tragedy. Talloran’s tragedy was a disaster woven together from personal psychological trauma, boundary violations in intimate relationships, the limitations of non-professional intervention, and prejudice within her family of origin. Those who look closely may notice that Talloran’s avatar was Gundam Exia from *Mobile Suit Gundam 00*, an image that also appeared on her final birthday cake. A central theme of *Mobile Suit Gundam 00* is that conflict between people can be resolved through communication and mutual understanding—something Talloran herself often spoke about with others during her lifetime. Yet reality proved otherwise. The failure of communication and the breakdown of relationships ultimately became the final straw that overwhelmed her. Perhaps this is also one of the most poignant aspects of this tragedy…
