## Introduction

Yuri, as described in her Bilibili bio, was initially an undiscovered trans girl. In her earlier days, she actively followed topics related to socialism, driven by a deep sense of idealism. She even composed a long [essay](https://www.bilibili.com/opus/503267259684512850) commemorating the 150th anniversary of the outbreak of the Paris Commune revolution.

Later on, she entered our community space, spending time with us in a warm and supportive environment, where she made many close and caring friends.

> “Aww, life gets really tough sometimes—here’s a big hug!”
>
> “I’m right here, pulling you into a warm embrace.”

In June 2024, Yuri began HRT, gradually transitioning to live as her true self. One evening, anxious yet hopeful, she tried to talk with her parents to test the waters, only to discover that her mother had already seen through it all along and quietly protected her secret. Even though the resistance from her father and other relatives never fully vanished, this small spark of understanding made her partially out to her family, giving her a touch of warmth in a cold world.

Yet despite this, her mental state didn’t improve. Severe depression and anxiety shadowed her every step. Even after spending an exhausting, full day navigating hospital bureaucracy, she couldn’t get the diagnosis certificate of gender dysphoria she so desperately needed.

Caught in a painful storm of extreme insecurity and a desperate longing for affection, she frequently found herself swept up in emotional turmoil. She tried various extreme or compensatory ways to escape the crushing weight of reality and to reach for even the faintest emotional connection—at one point even ending up in the emergency room.
