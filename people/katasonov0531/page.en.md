## Sister

I met Kata in Kapai’s group chat. She was one of Kapai’s several little sisters at the time—not by blood, but by choice. Since I later also became Kapai’s little sister, and Kata was a bit younger than me, she naturally became my little sister too.

But I noticed while chatting in the group that she wasn’t doing well, and I thought about whether I should add her and try talking to her, see if it might help a little. Even though my own situation wasn’t much better, given the complicated reasons at home and at school—but at least, I thought, at least we could help each other a bit.

After I added her as a friend, I finally understood just how bad her situation had become: her family of origin not only controlled her obsessively, but cut off her living expenses, and even kicked her out of the house, refusing to let her come home.

Poor Kata, my sister, was almost left on the streets. She was still so young—what so-called respectable and legitimate work could she possibly do? In the end, she had no choice but to take on some sex work just to get by.

## First Meeting

Once, she took a client from Shanghai. I told her I’d pick her up at Pudong Airport (PVG)—after all, we were friends who’d chatted, and never mind rolling out the red carpet, she was travelling all the way from Beijing, so how could I not do my duty as a host? So I waited in the arrivals hall, standing on my tiptoes in the crowd, hoping to catch sight of that familiar yet unfamiliar figure.

After I picked up Kata, she and I talked about a lot of things. At that time, my situation was much worse than it is now—my parents not only refused to buy me HRT medication, but often berated me. I had no way out, and could only try to “take clients” myself. She gave me quite a bit of advice on taking clients—telling me which ones to take and which not to, what to watch out for, how to protect myself…

Well, even though she was younger than me… I had to admit, she was a senior in this regard.

Kata had also come to Shanghai to see Yangmiao. We came out of the terminal, called a car, and went together to see Yangmiao.

That was my first time meeting her in person. Though it was rushed beyond words, looking back now, it stirs a mix of feelings.

## Patch

Once, Kata said she wanted to kill herself. At that time, we couldn’t reach her, and could only watch her updates anxiously. Later, after discussing it with Luoyiyu and the others, we decided to call the police.

It was an afternoon with still-bright sunshine. I was in class at school, but I still squeezed out the time to make that crucial call to the police. Fortunately, when the Beijing police found her, she hadn’t actually attempted suicide—she was just in a terrible mood, drinking alone. But this police officer, whether out of malice or just being rigidly by-the-book, had her taken to the station for a statement even though she was already safe. And because of this, when her father found out, he flew into a rage and kept threatening to send her to a psychiatric hospital. My sister… she was so scared, and I was scared too.

“Can you please not send her to a psychiatric hospital… She said if she gets locked up in there, she’ll try to kill herself…” I tried to communicate with her family. Then I comforted her: “Sister, come to Shanghai. At least you can stay with me… You won’t be sent away, you won’t be homeless.”

At the time, the bank card I used for tuition had just had the tuition money deposited. After setting aside the part for tuition, there was still some leftover, so I urgently bought her a plane ticket from Beijing to Shanghai.

She arrived in Shanghai on a Friday—right after I finished school. I picked her up at the airport as promised and settled her in at my home.

On Saturday evening, my mother took her out for a meal. Because I had fallen ill, my mother’s attitude toward me had finally had to change somewhat, and she naturally felt sympathy for a poor girl like Kata, so she made an appointment for her with a doctor at Xinhua Hospital.

On Sunday, I called up my middle school classmates and took her out to hang out. We walked along the Bund for a long time. Though we didn’t say much, as we walked, I couldn’t help but kiss her once. Later, when we went to see the Shanghai Three-Piece Set[^1], I couldn’t help but kiss her again. It was as if that could sustain some warmth that kept welling up inside.

I remember Kata stayed at my home for what felt like several days, but looking back now, it was nothing more than an all-too-short weekend.

> “Kata, why don’t you just stay here with me… It’s just that my school is very far from my home, about 30 kilometres. From Monday to Friday, I have to stay at school… I’ll have to go back tonight.”
>
> “It’s too lonely being alone at your place… I don’t think I can do that…”

At the time, I had already let her stay at my home alone, but she wouldn’t agree, and I had no choice but to let it be and let her arrange things herself.

## A Long Night

Somehow, she took a bottle of amantadine I had bought, still unopened… There should have been a bottle of pregabalin too, but in the end, I couldn’t snatch it back from her.

“Pregabalin and amantadine absolutely must not be taken together…” I couldn’t argue with her, so that was all I could say.

On the evening before I had to return to school, I had a meal with her—spicy hot pot… no, wait, not spicy hot pot, but maocai.

That night she took a lot of pregabalin, and I took some too. After all, I had school the next day, and I was so worried, so worried about her, the stress was too much—I still had to take it. It felt like there was no other way.

“Kata, please remember—pr and goodnight[^2]… you can’t take them together…” I warned her again and again.

Because I had to go to school, I couldn’t see her off at the airport in person. Late into the night before the next day, she flew back to Beijing. Before takeoff, she sent me a video taken from the aeroplane window, with her voice recorded in it.

“Your voice is really nice…”

“You can go to NZ with Sis Kapai… Good luck~w”

That’s what I wrote in the chat box.

But what I didn’t know was that on the night I was sound asleep in my dormitory, at around one in the morning, she swallowed that entire bottle—a full hundred pills of amantadine.

> “I’m sorry… I took the pills… I don’t know if I’ll live to see tomorrow. If I don’t reply for several days, just send out my obituary.”

It wasn’t until I woke up around six that I saw that message—the one I regretted not seeing in time, the one I couldn’t reply to in time…

## Afterword

> Are you okay now
>
> Kata, I miss you…
>
> I miss you…
>
> I miss you…

The frozen chat window, the phone number that can never be reached again, the updates that will never come—perhaps everything about the ending is already clear.

It was only until July of the following year that I truly learned from another friend that she had passed away, and only in December did that obituary arrive, more than a year late.

Kata, my sister, was a good person. Even though she was younger than me… she was indeed a senior when it came to taking clients… Even now, I still feel grateful to her.

She was still so young, so young…

To this day, her profile page still displays my earliest ID, but she doesn’t know that I’ve been banned seven more times since then, and that bio of hers of course can never be updated again.

I didn’t meet Kata in person many times, and I didn’t know her very deeply. But she truly was a lovely, kind person, and she will always be my dear sister.

My dear, dear sister, goodnight. In that world, may there be no more pain…
